KYC verification

KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory identity verification procedure that all licensed casinos go through. At Lucky Bear, verification is required for withdrawing large amounts and for full access to the cashier functionality. The procedure includes uploading a scan of an identity document (passport, ID card or driver's license) and confirming your residential address.

Verification usually takes from 15 minutes to 24 hours. After successfully passing KYC, withdrawal limits are increased and request processing is accelerated. All documents are stored encrypted and are not transferred to third parties. This is standard practice that protects both the player and the platform from fraud.
