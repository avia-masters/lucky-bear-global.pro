Wagering and terms

Wagering is a coefficient showing how many times you need to roll over the bonus funds before you can withdraw them. For example, with a wagering of x40 and a bonus of 10,000 ₽, you need to place bets totaling 400,000 ₽. It is important to consider not only the coefficient, but also the wagering period, max bet and game contribution — these parameters are specified in the terms of each promotion.

Before activating any bonus, carefully study the exclusion table: some slots do not participate in wagering, and table games and live casino give only partial contribution. Violation of terms (for example, exceeding the max bet) may lead to cancellation of the bonus and winnings from it.
