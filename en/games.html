RTP and volatility

RTP (Return to Player) is the percentage of return to the player from the total amount of bets. The higher the RTP, the greater the theoretical return. For example, an RTP of 96% means that out of every 100 ₽ wagered in the long term, the player gets back 96 ₽, and 4 ₽ remains with the casino. This is a statistical indicator, not a guarantee of winning in a short session.

Volatility is the degree of risk of a slot. Low volatility gives frequent but small wins. High — rare but large. Lucky Bear features slots of all volatility types, which allows you to choose a game for your style and bankroll size.

Strategies and bankroll management

Bankroll management is a key skill for any player. The main rule: never bet more than you can afford to lose. It is recommended to allocate a separate bankroll for the gaming session and not exceed it. The optimal bet size is 1–2% of the bankroll.

  • Set limits in advance. Establish a maximum loss and win amount per session.
  • Don't chase losses. Attempts to win back usually lead to even greater losses.
  • Use bonuses. The welcome package and cashback increase play time and chances of winning.
  • Study RTP. Choose slots with RTP above 96% for more favorable conditions.
  • Take breaks. Prolonged play reduces concentration and increases the risk of impulsive decisions.
